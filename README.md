## Project Overview
This repository contains my Digital Literacy project completed as part of the CSE0001 course. The objective of this project was to understand how digital tools are used in academic and professional environments, and how students can develop a responsible and effective digital presence.

The project is divided into five tasks, each focusing on a different aspect of digital literacy such as awareness, online presence, coding platforms, communication, and cyber safety. All tasks have been completed using simple online tools and are organized in separate folders.


## Task Summaries

### Task 1: Digital Literacy Infographic
In this task, I created an infographic using Canva to explain the concept of digital literacy. The design includes key areas such as safe internet practices, useful digital tools, professional online presence, and email etiquette. The aim was to present the information in a simple and easy-to-understand format.

Folder: task-1-presentation/


### Task 2: Digital Portfolio
For this task, I created and updated my profiles on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle. These platforms are important for building a professional identity and showcasing skills. This task helped me understand how to present my academic background and interests in a professional way.

Folder: task-2-portfolio/


### Task 3: Coding and Collaboration Platforms
In this task, I explored coding and collaboration tools. I completed a beginner-level problem on HackerRank and created a Google Form titled "Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz" with five questions. This helped me understand how such tools can be used for learning and collecting responses.

Google Form Link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtm45HuL4MYIxqU-tx184bygzr_5K-WtBWOxHIlrMObko1LA/viewform?usp=header 

Folder: task-3-platforms/


### Task 4: Email Etiquette
This task involved writing two professional emails and preparing a checklist of do’s and don’ts for social media usage. It helped me understand the importance of clear and respectful communication in a professional environment.

Folder: task-4-email-etiquette/


### Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness
In this task, I worked on a case study related to phishing and created a prevention checklist. This task increased my awareness about online threats and the importance of following safe digital practices.

Folder: task-5-cybercrime/
